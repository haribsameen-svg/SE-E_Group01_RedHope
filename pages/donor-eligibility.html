Eligibility Guidelines (Yes & No)

Yes (Eligible) No (Not Eligible)
Age between 18 and 65 years Under 18 or over 65 years old
Body weight at least 50 kg Body weight under 50 kg
Feeling healthy and well on donation day Fever, cold, flu, or active infection
At least 56 days since last whole-blood donation Donated blood within the last 56 days
Healthy hemoglobin & normal blood pressure Low hemoglobin (anemia) or uncontrolled BP
No surgery, tattoo, or piercing in the last 6 months Recent major surgery, tattoo, or piercing (< 6 months)

Good to confirm

If you are unsure, always confirm.

Consult a Red Hope coordinator or your doctor before donating if you take regular medication or have a medical condition.

Quick Eligibility Test

1. Are you between 18 and 65 years old?

2. Do you weigh at least 50 kg?

3. Are you feeling healthy and free of fever, cold, or infection today?

4. Has it been at least 56 days since your last blood donation (or never donated)?

5. Have you avoided major surgery, tattoos, or piercings in the last 6 months?
