Terms & Conditions
1. Eligibility

Donors must be at least 18 years of age, weigh at least 50 kg, and be in good general health. Specific medical conditions may disqualify a donor; a Red Hope coordinator will verify eligibility before any donation session.

2. Data Usage

Your personal information (name, phone, email, city, blood group) will be stored securely and used solely to connect you with patients who need your blood type. Red Hope will never sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Voluntary Participation

All blood donations arranged through Red Hope are strictly voluntary. You may withdraw your registration at any time by contacting help@redhope.pk.

4. Accuracy of Information

By submitting this form you confirm that all information provided is accurate. Providing false information may result in removal from the donor network.

5. Limitation of Liability

Red Hope acts as an intermediary platform. We do not conduct medical procedures and are not liable for any direct or indirect harm arising from donations arranged through the platform.

6. Contact

For questions, write to help@redhope.pk or call 051-1234567.
